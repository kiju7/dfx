custom discovery paths; the documentation now says `--skills-dir` must point to
a directory already scanned by the host.

unavailable-model fallback, shared-file conflict isolation, or repair-loop
convergence has been established by these smoke tests. The remaining scenarios
in [scenarios.md](scenarios.md) are evaluation plans, not passed tests.
